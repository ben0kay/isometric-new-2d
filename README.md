# Isometric Alien Survival — Godot Project

> **Development status:** Early development / active prototyping

A 2D isometric science-fiction survival game set on an alien world, developed using **Godot 4 and C#**.

The project focuses on building a large, explorable world with distinct biomes, environmental systems, entities, vegetation, and persistent world state.

Development currently emphasizes creating a scalable, modular gameplay foundation before expanding the game's visual content and survival mechanics.

---

## Game Overview

Explore an alien environment filled with unfamiliar vegetation, varied terrain, creatures, and changing environmental conditions.

The long-term vision is a survival experience where exploration, environmental hazards, and interactions with the world are central to gameplay.

## Core Systems

### World Generation
- Procedural world generation and chunk-based streaming.
- Multiple environmental biome types.
- Terrain and vegetation placement systems.
- World loading and unloading around the player.

### Vegetation and Environment
- Biome-specific plants and trees.
- Support for multiple sprite variations.
- Data-driven vegetation configuration.
- Environmental lighting and shadow rendering.

### Entities and Population
- Entity management and movement systems.
- Population management across world regions.
- Systems designed to accommodate future creature behaviours.

### World Persistence
- Saving and restoring world state.
- Tracking persistent changes across chunk loading and unloading.
- Persistence support for world objects and dropped items.
- Ongoing development of save-system reliability and extensibility.

### Development Tools
- Custom debugging utilities.
- Biome content configuration tools.
- Sprite asset preparation and batch-processing workflows.
- Performance inspection and troubleshooting utilities.

## Development Roadmap

The project is actively evolving. Current and upcoming areas of development include:

- Stabilizing world-state persistence.
- Expanding biome-specific environmental content.
- Improving entity behaviours and interactions.
- Adding survival gameplay mechanics.
- Replacing placeholder visuals with original sprite artwork.
- Expanding user interface, audio, and environmental feedback.
- Continued performance profiling and optimization.

These are development goals, not a guarantee that every feature is currently playable.
